## Cross-cutting themes (from all 10 experts)

1. **Community before promotion** — Value-first comments; 9:1 or 90/10 contribution ratios.
2. **Account warm-up** — 30–90 days karma building before brand mentions.
3. **Reddit → Google → AI** — Threads rank in SERPs and feed ChatGPT/Perplexity citations.
4. **Social listening** — F5Bot, Reddit Pro, keyword tables (brand, category, competitors).
5. **B2B SaaS fit** — High-intent "best tool for X" threads; PLG and reputation management use cases.
6. **Measurement** — Brand search lift, AI citation tracking, assisted conversions—not last-click ROAS.
