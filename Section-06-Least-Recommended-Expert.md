# Competing Opinions: Same Reddit Marketing Issues, Different Answers

Compares **contrasting viewpoints** among the ten profiled experts on shared strategic questions.

In-text citations: APA 7th edition. Full references: [COMPILATION-references-apa.md](./COMPILATION-references-apa.md).

## Issue 1: How Fast Should You Expect Results?

| Viewpoint | Who holds it | Core argument |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| **Fast / tactical wins possible** | Dekker the Marketer (Fraser, 2026a); MD Noor Alom (Alom, 2025) | Find Google-ranking Reddit threads, comment strategically, and see visibility within days; single native posts can drive 190K+ views and first customers quickly. |
| **Medium-term (90 days structured)** | Brent Csutoras (Csutoras, 2025a); Krista Doyle (Superpath, 2026) | Month-by-month onboarding builds credibility; 90-day warmup is standard, with small wins around week 2 but strategic impact compounding after. |
| **Long-term (6–12+ months)** | Brent Csutoras (Csutoras, 2025a); Krista Doyle (Superpath, 2026); Ahsan Zahid (Zahid, 2026d); Kartik Mahajan (Wellput, n.d.) | Meaningful Reddit impact typically needs 6–12 months—or months of consistent 90/10 engagement before payoff. |

**Decision guide:** Need AI/SERP presence this quarter? → Ranking-thread strategy (Fraser, 2026a; Davies, 2026b). Building enterprise reputation? → 90-day+ program (Csutoras, 2025a; Superpath, 2026).

## Issue 2: Organic Community Building vs. SEO/GEO Thread Targeting

| Viewpoint | Who holds it | Core argument |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| **Community-first; SEO is a byproduct** | Brent Csutoras (Csutoras, 2025a); Krista Doyle (Doyle, 2026b); MD Noor Alom (Alom, 2026a) | Show up as humans, solve problems—SEO and AI citations follow trust. |
| **SEO/GEO-first; reverse-engineer citations** | Jules Davies (Davies, 2026b); Tayyaba Ameen (Ameen, 2026c); Devesh Paliwal (Paliwal, 2026b); Oleg G. (Galeev, 2026) | Target ranking and LLM-cited threads; structure content for Google and AI extraction. |

|-----------|--------------|---------------|
