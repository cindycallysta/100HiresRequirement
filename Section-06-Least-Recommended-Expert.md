# Who I Wouldn't Recommend Following for Reddit Marketing (Specifically as a Beginner)

While evaluating growth marketers and organic strategy accounts across X, Kmahjn (@kmahjn) is an account I would advise beginners to pass by when looking to learn Reddit Marketing. Although their content may appeal to advanced operators looking for growth hacks, here is why their approach is counterproductive and confusing for newcomers. 

---

## Indirect and Vague Playbooks

| The Problem | Why is it not Suitable for Beginners |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Their advice tends to focus on high-level and indirect framing rather than a concreate and repeatable workflow.** | Beginners need foundational clarity: how to build account karma safely, navigate strict AutoMod rules, understand subreddit culture, and avoid shadowbans. Vague or indirect recommendations leave too much ambiguity, often leading beginners to misinterpret tactics and get banned immediately. | 

---

## High "Hustle or Tool-First" Bias over Subreddit Psychology

| The Problem | Why is it not Suitable for Beginners |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Content often centers on rapid distribution hacks, automation stacks, and leveraging cross-platform tools to scale reach fast.** | Reddit is the most anti-automation, anti-promotional platform on the internet. Applying Twitter/X growth tactics (high volume, quick hooks, indirect funneling) directly to Reddit backfires. Beginners need to learn community-native nuance and organic contribution before touching any advanced growth or distribution playbooks.

---

## Skewed Toward Product Promotion Rather Than Organic Retention

| The Problem | Why is it not Suitable for Beginners |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Much of the shared strategy focuses on using Reddit as a top-of-funnel traffic tap for external products/offers rather than building sustainable, authentic brand equity.** | Without an intuitive grasp of how moderators think and how Redditors detect covert ads, executing indirect promotional schemes almost always results in deleted threads, lost account reputation, and wasted effort. |
