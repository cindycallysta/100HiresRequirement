## Summary

## Key transcript excerpts

**Fan Out thesis:**
> What fanout does is help B2B brands get visible in AI search specifically through the channels that most marketing teams aren't really paying attention to right now—Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn.

**Background:** 10+ years SEO/content/community @ HubSpot, Jasper, Twitter; co-founded Chalant (fandom marketing app)—fused fandom + SEO for AI search era.

**LinkedIn for B2B citations:**
- Fastest-growing AI citation source over past year
- Perplexity indexes LinkedIn posts; ChatGPT cites professional expertise
- Personal profiles > company pages for citation pathways

**Reddit role (implicit throughout):**
- Part of "reputation layer" where 85% of AI brand mentions originate
- Off-site strategy: identify conversations → fan out authentically → enable employees/advocates

**Framework: Identify → Fan Out → Enable**
1. Map where buyers research (Reddit threads, G2, YouTube comments)
2. Build authentic presence in those conversations
3. Enable internal leaders + power users as advocates
