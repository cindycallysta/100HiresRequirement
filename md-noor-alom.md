## Post 3: AI Listens to Reddit — Founder Playbook

**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/md-noor-alom-1004ab188_as-a-marketer-i-have-no-excuse-to-ignore-activity-7449534554559885312-G0dJ

**Why Reddit matters for AI:**
1. Depth — long problem/solution threads
2. Diversity — niche domain expertise
3. Authenticity — votes surface what works
4. Freshness — real-time pain points
5. Authority — subreddits = micro knowledge hubs for LLMs

**5-step playbook:**
1. Listen — 5-10 subreddits, monitor top threads
2. Learn — capture questions and objections
3. Help genuinely — answer, don't pitch
4. Seed value — AMAs, case studies, follow-ups
5. Repurpose — FAQs, docs, SEO pages AI will use
