## Notes on APA Formatting

1. **Social media posts:** APA 7 allows citing individual posts with author, date, first 20 words of post (or descriptive title in italics), site name, and URL (American Psychological Association, 2020).

2. **LinkedIn profiles (n.d.):** Use “n.d.” when no publication date appears on the profile page.

3. **YouTube videos:** Credit the uploader (channel name) as author; include video title in sentence case and URL.

4. **Access dates:** Not required for stable URLs unless content is designed to change (APA, 2020). LinkedIn post URLs may require login; archive posts if needed for academic submission.

5. **KMahjn (@kmahjn):** X profile at https://x.com/kmahjn could not be accessed during compilation. Add specific post citations when available.
